Voltage drop

When a load draws current, any unwanted series resistance develops a voltage drop equal to current times resistance. The lamp then receives less voltage and may dim.

Short-circuit protection

A low-resistance bypass draws high current and can open a fuse. The simulation uses a simplified 15 A threshold, not a fuse time-current curve or real ECU/transient model.
