Example 6-speed manual gearbox. This is not Volvo I-Shift geometry or an OEM gear chart.

Why the clutch matters

Pressing the pedal separates the friction surfaces. The engine can then turn without driving the gearbox input at full speed. A synchronizer or proper speed matching is required for shifts.

What the gear ratio means

An example ratio of 4.50:1 means 4.5 input rotations for one output rotation. Friction losses, slip, tooth geometry and full vehicle inertia are not resolved here.
