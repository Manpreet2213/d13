Why the air spring matters

The compressed air supports the load. Higher pressure can raise the frame, but real height control and spring rate depend on bag geometry, load, and height-control valves.

Why the shock absorber matters

The damper resists suspension velocity and dissipates oscillation energy. Remove damping and repeated bumps persist longer. This is a one-dimensional teaching model.
